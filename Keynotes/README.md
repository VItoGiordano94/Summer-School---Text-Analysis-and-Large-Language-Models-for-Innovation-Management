# Keynote Speakers

This folder contains the materials shared by the **keynote speakers** invited to the **Summer School on Text Analysis and Large Language Models for Innovation Management**, organised by the University of Pisa.

The keynote sessions complement the main lessons and practical activities of the Summer School by providing participants with expert perspectives on emerging developments, methodological challenges, and academic perspective on text analysis, Natural Language Processing, and Large Language Models.

Here the list of keynote speakers:

- **Ivan Zupic**, Senior Lecturer at the Institute of Management Studies, Goldsmiths, University of London; Presentation title: A Human-Centered Workflow for Using Large Language Models in Content Analysis (Link to use during the keynote: https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fo/8zdg4kxq7mq15p392wxa1/AJbhN33iKuYMGSKS1gtG890?rlkey=1u0kq82bcueckzm620fqwe1p3&st=oimqsqfc&dl=0)
- **Andrea Bonaccorsi**, Full Professor at the University of Pisa; Presentation title: From Text to Action: Challenges for Innovation, Management, and Society
- **Jane Lê**, Professor at WHU – Otto Beisheim School of Management; Presentation title: AI in Research Methods: An Innovation Too Far?
- **Stefan Feuerriegel**, Professor and Head of the Institute of AI in Management, LMU Munich School of Management; Presentation title: A Consensus-Based Reporting Checklist for LLMs in Behavioral and Social Science

## Use of the Materials

The materials are intended primarily for participants in the Summer School and may be used for educational and research purposes.

When referring to or reusing specific content, participants should appropriately acknowledge the keynote speaker, the presentation title, and the **Summer School on Text Analysis and Large Language Models for Innovation Management**.
